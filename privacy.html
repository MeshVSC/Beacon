Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The Short Version

Nexus does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We have no accounts, no registration, no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking. Your privacy is not a feature we sell -- it is the default.

Data We Collect

None. Nexus is a fully client-side static website. There is no server processing your requests, no database storing your information, and no backend of any kind.

Local Storage

Nexus uses your browser's localStorage to save your preferences locally on your device. This includes:

  • Star ratings you give to tools
  • Language preference (EN/ES/CA)
  • Any custom tools or category configurations
  • Admin mode settings (if enabled)

This data never leaves your browser. It is not sent to any server, not shared with anyone, and is fully under your control. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Cookies

Nexus does not set any cookies. We do not use cookie-based tracking, session cookies, or any cookie consent mechanisms because there are no cookies to consent to.

Analytics & Tracking

Nexus does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics or tracking service. We do not track page views, clicks, user behavior, or any other metrics. We have no idea who you are or what you do on this site.

Third-Party Services

Nexus uses the following third-party services:

  • Thum.io -- for generating website screenshot thumbnails. When a screenshot is displayed, your browser requests the image from thum.io's servers. Their privacy policy applies to that request.
  • Google Favicons -- for displaying small website icons. Your browser requests these from Google's servers.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN -- for styling. Loaded from cdn.tailwindcss.com.
  • Google Fonts -- for the Geist typeface. Loaded from fonts.googleapis.com.

These services may log standard web request data (IP address, user agent) according to their own privacy policies. Nexus has no control over this.

Third-Party Links

Nexus is a directory of links to third-party websites. When you click a link and leave Nexus, you are subject to that website's privacy policy and terms. We strongly recommend reviewing the privacy practices of any tool before providing it with personal information.

GDPR Compliance

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Nexus does not process personal data as defined by the GDPR
  • No data controller or data processor relationship exists because no personal data is collected
  • There is no personal data to access, rectify, erase, or port
  • No consent mechanism is required because there is nothing to consent to

If you believe any of the above is incorrect or have concerns about your data, please contact us.

Children's Privacy

Nexus does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The NSFW section of the catalog contains links to adult content and should not be accessed by minors.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices (which would mean we actually started collecting data), this policy will be updated accordingly. Given our architecture, this is unlikely.

Contact
